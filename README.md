# moonlight

*moon phases, horary readings and a quiet little sky report — astrology that respects your lock screen.*

## what is this?
an iOS app for people who glance at the moon before making plans. it tracks moon phases, casts charts, and gives you readings — plus one small daily sky reading on the home screen, like a weather report for your inner life.

## features
- moon phase tracking with lock screen and home widgets (TR/EN)
- natal and now charts, cached kindly so slow upstreams never block you
- readings with full history — tap any old reading to reopen it
- daily sky reading card, one per day
- credits and a monthly moonlight+ subscription
- birth data you can edit or delete entirely; privacy manifest included

## technologies
- SwiftUI, widgets via WidgetKit
- a worker proxy in front of the astrology API, with app-side chart caching
- StoreKit for credits and subscription

## where this is going
nearly at the App Store — icon, store products and a final check stand between here and submit.
