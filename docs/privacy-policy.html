Privacy Policy - Moonlight

Last updated: July 10, 2026

What we collect

  • Birth chart info (birth date, time and place, and your optional first name): Stored locally on your device. Your birth moment and place are sent to our astrology calculation service each time a chart is computed, and your name and signs may be included in AI reading requests to personalise the text. Neither is stored on our servers after the response is delivered.
  • Location: Used to calculate accurate moon phase data for your area. Not stored or shared.
  • Questions you ask: Sent to our AI service to generate readings. Not stored after the reading is delivered.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your email or phone number, and we have no user accounts.
  • We don't track your activity across other apps.
  • We don't sell any data to third parties.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Your questions are processed by Claude AI to generate readings. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this processing.
  • FreeAstrologyAPI: Your approximate location and current time are used to calculate planetary positions. No personal data is shared.
  • Apple StoreKit: In-app purchases are handled by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Data storage

All personal preferences (birth chart info, credit balance) are stored locally on your device using iOS built-in storage. We have no server-side database of user data.

Children

Moonlight is not intended for children under 13.

Your rights (KVKK / GDPR)

Under Turkish data protection law (KVKK) and the GDPR you can access, correct and erase your personal data at any time. Because Moonlight keeps your data on your device, you exercise these rights directly in the app: edit your birth info from the Birth Chart screen, or use Settings > Delete My Data to erase your birth info, reading history and cached charts instantly. Deleting the app removes everything else. For anything you cannot do in the app, contact us at the address below.

Contact
